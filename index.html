Website-Based Lost and Found Tracking System for Students

BSHS LOST AND FOUND HUB helps students report, search, track, and recover lost belongings through a secure and easy-to-use online platform designed for schools and universities.

Report Item Claim Item

System Features

A student-friendly platform that improves the traditional lost and found process by making item reporting and tracking faster, organized, and accessible online.

Lost Item Reporting

Students can submit details of lost belongings including item name, category, date, location, description, and photo.

Found Item Registration

Students or staff can register found items so that owners can easily search and claim them.

Search and Filtering

Users can search for items by category, location, date, status, or keyword to quickly find matching reports.

Claim Request

Owners can send a claim request and provide proof of ownership before the item is returned.

Admin Verification

Administrators can verify reports, approve claims, manage users, and prevent false or duplicate submissions.

Status Tracking

Each report has a status such as pending, matched, claimed, or returned, making the recovery process transparent.

How It Works

BSHS LOST AND FOUND HUB follows a simple process to connect students who lost items with students or staff who found them.

1

Report

A student submits a lost or found item report using the online form.

2

Match

The system compares item details such as category, location, date, color, and description.

3

Verify

The administrator checks the claim and confirms the rightful owner.

4

Return

Once approved, the item is safely returned to the student.

About BSHS LOST AND FOUND HUB


BSHS LOST AND FIND HUB is a website-based Lost and Found Tracking System created for students, teachers, and school administrators. Its main goal is to replace manual lost and found records with a digital platform that allows fast reporting, easy searching, claim verification, and secure item return. The system supports better organization, transparency, and convenience within the school community.
